### Stage 10 — Institutions, Governance, Law, Conflict Resolution (Tier 3)

Classification — build-surface: leadership/authority/legitimacy records,
succession, councils and collective-decision records, rules and laws as
records, dispute resolution, restitution, enforcement-as-committed-
consequence, offices and roles, communal property, public contributions,
public works, taxation (scope decision). Measured-outcome candidates:
legitimacy dynamics, corruption, favouritism, resistance, rebellion —
expected to emerge from records + incentives; each gets a probe-decided call
(mechanism seam vs measured outcome) at contract time. Expected deferrals:
none pre-declared; war/peace at scale may defer pending Stage 11 population
dynamics — probe-decided.

Provisional carve: **10A authority & legitimacy** (formalising the 7D
coordinator lineage; succession) → **10B rules & collective decisions**
(councils, laws, offices) → **10C dispute, restitution, enforcement,
communal property / public works / taxation** → **10D external relations &
organised conflict** (diplomacy, treaties, rebellion, war and peace).

Consumes: 9C ownership (restitution, communal property, theft adjudication),
9D value (taxation, contributions), 8C violation detection + relationship
consequences (dispute genesis), 7D group goals. Produces: authority/law
registries (→ 11 inheritance disputes, → 12 institutional history).

Open decisions: (1) **the boundary-field relocation** — `authority`, `law`,
`punishment` enter as institution registries while remaining forbidden in
culture records; the relocation contract (protocol invariant 7) is 10A's
first deliverable; (2) **enforcement vs obedience** — enforcement is
committed consequence on records and relationships, never command
automation; the survival-dominance guard extends to institutional influence;
(3) taxation "where appropriate" scope. Activation: Stage 9 close-out with
the un-inerting gate resolved.

### Stage 11 — Ecology, Demography, Lifecycles, World-Scale Change (Tier 3)

Classification — build-surface: the reproduction chain (relationships →
fertility → conception → pregnancy → birth), development and ageing,
illness/injury/disability, death, family lines, **inheritance execution**
(the ratified landing of the 9/11 dual-listing), migration and movement,
settlement growth/decline/abandonment, predator–prey, plant and resource
cycles, seasons (extend the existing weather domain), disease spread,
depletion/recovery, disasters, climate pressure. Measured outcomes:
population bottlenecks, technological spread, cultural spread at demographic
scale (rides 8D diffusion). Roadmap constraint: population stays
lifecycle-driven — no target-population spawning or balancing mechanics.

Provisional carve: **11A lifecycle core** (birth→death, family lines,
inheritance) → **11B health & disease** → **11C movement & settlements** →
**11D ecology & climate**.

Consumes: 9C ownership (inheritance), Stage 10 optionally (inheritance
disputes). Unblocks the demography-blocked deferrals (8B generational
transfer lands at 11A). Produces: the population turnover Stage 12 requires
— the turnover that makes "culture outlives its originators" real at scale.

Open decisions: (1) **cap policy vs population growth** — protocol
invariant 4 ("never raise a prior stage's cap") meets a growing population;
expected resolution is new lifecycle registries with their own caps and
deterministic compaction, or an explicitly ratified cap contract — decided
at the 11A contract STOP, never ad hoc; (2) **horizon strategy** — lifecycle
spans exceed 1,000-tick scenarios; the standing scenario decision is
expected to reopen here by design. Activation: Stage 10 close-out (or a
ratified 10-partial gate if 10D defers).

### Stage 12 — Integrated Historical Burn-In (Tier 3)

Classification — build-surface: long-run harness mode, performance/
throughput/storage telemetry, burn-in configurations, emergence-checklist
audit tooling, deterministic long-run stress tests. Measured outcomes: the
entire history list (family histories, grudges, debts, trade routes, wars,
lost knowledge, extinct groups, institutions with traceable origins) —
burn-in is graded on emergence quality, not tick count (roadmap rule).

Provisional carve: **12A performance & scale baseline** (throughput, hash
cost, Mongo behaviour, cap headroom at 10^5–10^6 ticks) → **12B staged
burn-ins** against the measured emergence checklist → **12C stress &
failure modes** (bottlenecks, collapse, extinction) → **12D history-quality
audit & acceptance**.

Consumes: Stages 9–11 complete (roadmap: meaningful history requires the
earlier systems) and **un-inerted culture** — a hard precondition; inert
culture makes burn-in history socially hollow. Produces: the canonical
history volume Stage 13 requires; the long-run determinism proof.

Open decisions: what "long periods" is numerically (probe-decided at 12A);
retention and storage-growth policy handoff to Stage 13. Risk owner for the
performance wall — mitigated by the protocol's from-Stage-9 telemetry rule.

### Stage 13 — Historical Compression & World Legibility (Tier 3)

Build-surface: significance scoring, event summarisation, historical
periods/eras, biographies (entity, family, settlement, faction, cultural,
economic, territorial, institutional), drill-down from summaries to accepted
events, first-divergence inspection, why-is-the-world-like-this queries,
bounded diagnostic retention, durable preservation of major events. Roadmap
constraint: compression never replaces accepted events as the source of
truth.

Provisional carve: **13A significance & summarisation core** → **13B
biography/history projections** → **13C causal drill-down &
first-divergence** → **13D retention & preservation policy**.

Consumes: Stage 12's history volume — significance scoring cannot be tuned
on toy histories. Produces: the legibility layer Stages 14/15 require.

Open decisions: read-model architecture — this is the first stage where
projections become the primary build surface; the **frontend baseline
audit** (parked-work register) is scheduled no later than 13 kickoff.

### Stage 14 — Player Entry & Embodiment (Tier 3)

Build-surface: origin selection/generation, embodiment through the same
proposal spine, household/faction context, inherited relationships,
perception and knowledge boundaries, player memory, exposure to ownership/
law/cultural expectation, mortality and consequences, world-reaction parity.
Roadmap constraints: no separate player reality; player entry never resets
or invalidates existing history.

Provisional carve: **14A origin & embodiment substrate** → **14B perception
& knowledge boundaries** → **14C player action through the canonical
spine** → **14D consequence-parity audit** (player rules ≡ agent rules,
proven by test).

Consumes: Stage 13 legibility; every prior system, for parity; ADR-001 fork
semantics (player-world lineage). Open decision: **interaction cadence** —
player input vs the deterministic tick (pause-per-tick, batched intents, or

dedicated design STOP no later than the Stage 13 close-out.

### Stage 15 — Player-Facing Interaction & Narrative Projection (Tier 3)

Provisional carve: **15A projection surfaces** → **15B interaction &

## Pre-flight dependency friction (Stages 9–15)

The architectural break each future stage will hit, named now so it is planned into the stage's contract rather than re-invented at implementation. Two lines per stage: the **hard dependency blocker** (what must be true before the stage starts) and the **known architectural friction** (the structural break to design for in advance). Speculative until each stage's evidence-grounded contract supersedes it.

- **Stage 9 (Economy).** Blocker: Stage 8 core loop closes. Friction: a schema break from Stage 7's `shared_storage` — today inventory is strictly per-person carry; Stage 9 needs storage containers, ownership records/locks, and surplus accounting. Ownership is a record, never authority; the conservation invariant means stocks must re-derive exactly from committed event history. Plan the 7→9 storage migration path now, not at 9A.
- **Stage 10 (Institutions).** Blocker: Stage 9 close-out with the un-inerting gate resolved. Friction: the boundary-field relocation — `authority`, `law`, `punishment` are *forbidden* in culture records but become institution registries here. The forbidden-fields invariant changes meaning at this boundary and needs an explicit relocation contract (10A's first deliverable). Enforcement stays committed-consequence on records/relationships, never command automation; the survival-dominance guard extends to institutional influence.
- **Stage 11 (Ecology/Demography).** Blocker: Stage 10 close-out (or a ratified 10-partial gate). Friction: invariant 4 ("never raise a prior stage's cap") collides with a growing population — resolve with new lifecycle registries carrying their own caps, never by raising an old cap. Lifecycle spans exceed the 1,000-tick scenario horizon, so the standing scenario decision reopens here by design.
- **Stage 12 (Burn-In).** Blocker: Stages 9–11 complete and culture un-inerted (inert culture makes burn-in history socially hollow). Friction: the performance wall — throughput, hash cost, and storage growth at 10^5–10^6 ticks are unmeasured. The from-Stage-9 telemetry rule exists to size this before 12A, not after.
- **Stage 13 (Legibility).** Blocker: Stage 12 history volume (significance scoring cannot be tuned on toy histories). Friction: read-model architecture — this is the first stage where projections become the primary build surface. The frontend baseline audit (parked-work register) must land by 13 kickoff.
- **Stage 14 (Player Embodiment).** Blocker: Stage 13 legibility, and every prior system for rule-parity. Friction: **player input is non-deterministic, and the engine's entire replay proof assumes keyed-RNG determinism.** Inserting player actions without breaking replay requires a keyed player-input side-channel in the commit pipeline — inputs recorded as ordered, replayable events, never live mutations. Interaction cadence (pause-per-tick / batched intents / real-time projection over a ticking core) is architecture-shaping and gets a dedicated design STOP by the Stage 13 close-out.
- **Stage 15 (Narrative).** Blocker: Stage 14. Friction: the LLM boundary — any generative narrative must be provably non-canonical (projection-layer only, no write-back path), proven by the truth-isolation audit. Generative-vs-templated is decided at the 15C contract, not before.
